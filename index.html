About Me

I am a veteran and a psychiatric registered nurse. I served in the U.S. Navy for five years before medically retiring. After my retirement, I searched for a career that could give me meaning and settled on nursing. I've been a nurse now for three years.

Education

I graduated from Milwaukee Job Corps Center in 2012 with a CNA certificate. In 2023, I graduated from ECPI University with an ADN- RN. In 2024, I graduated from Capella University with a BSN. I am now pursuing a Bachelor's degree in software engineering at University of Wisconsin- Green Bay.

Career

I started my career as an aviation electronics technician in the U.S. Navy. After medically retiring, I went on to pursue a career in nursing. I am now a registered nurse working in an inpatient psychiatric hospital.
